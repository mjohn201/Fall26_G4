## Discussion
We discussed the proposed layout and how students would move
between the three screens. The Grade Entry screen would provide
Calculate and Clear buttons. The results screen would allow
students to return and edit their grades.

We discussed calculating the course grade by multiplying each
category average by its weight and adding the results. We also
discussed the final exam goal feature, which uses the current
grade, final exam weight, and desired course grade.

## Proposed Input Rules
- Require numerical values in all necessary fields.
- Accept grades from 0 to 100.
- Require category weights to total 100%.
- Require the final exam goal weight to be greater than 0%
  and less than 100%.
- Explain when a required final exam score exceeds 100%.

## Proposed Grading Scale
A: 90–100
B: 80 to below 90
C: 70 to below 80
D: 60 to below 70
F: Below 60

The prototype should explain that students must check their
course syllabus for the correct grading scale.
